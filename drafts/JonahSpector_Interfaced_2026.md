## Part 1: Fees

### Chapter 1: Elliot: The Cipherists

<!-- Scene: kern-marez-0934 -->
<!-- Characters: Elliot, Manoj, Rafael, Habib, Anita-->
<!-- Date: December 1, 2103 -->

The server room Elliot Fetterman overlooked was molten, dry air perturbed by the
heat, no light other than the muted red glow of stacked metal. The entire array
was sunken down into the ground, his view obscured by the reinforced metal
framing of a thick acrylic window, tilted overlooking the expanse. Each node
flickered, and the whole valley of computers faded into the dark fog of the
background. It was a failing fight against entropy.

<!-- Because the efficiency gained by Embedded labor is outweighed by the war. -->

Elliot sat with one leg propped up on the windowsill, and the other idly
scraping the side of the wall as it swung. Lost in a waking dream, twice
embodied the doubleSignal gazed absentmindedly at the heat. He was connected by
portable neuralModem to a virtual reality experience called Dreamworld, a
Userland server owned by the Altetro Corporation, where little of himself
resided. Almost all of him was stuck in the dingy maintenance room, watching air
wobble, and *thinking*, to put it euphemistically. He heard a noise behind him,
but paid it no attention.

A rancid set of thoughtStreams played in his mind while he lazed on the
windowsill. *New Userland server listing available now at Freehub... Major
Tachi-Odyne--Altetro deal to be announced tomorrow... Popular IRL streamer
revealed as predator... Embedded labor strikes ended by force, two dead...
Tachi-Odyne stock down five points...* buzzed the autophony of a trained
voice-to-thoughtStream reporter. The mouthfeel of greasy food, pounding heart of
a solider in a fierce firefight, hurt of a police beating, focused thoughts of a
physicist at work, butterfly stomach of a public nudist, and more accompanied
the main news thoughtStream. Dozens of trains of thought, and mental copies from
the experienced reality of dozens of people, rich and poor, saturated Elliot's
mind, filling him with useless information he was sure to forget the next
instant. Elliot was thoughtless in that moment, his eyes moving across the
server room by no impulse of his own creation.

On top of the overload of thoughtStreams into his mind over non-Matrix
protocols, and his glassy-eyed observation of the IRL server room, he strolled,
hands in pocket, down the infinite upscale bar of Dreamworld, a virtual world
indistinguishable from reality by all senses. DoubleSignal Elliot, miraculously
managing to split himself thrice, let a stray thought squeeze out, *Focus. Like
half IRL, forty stream, ten Matrix. Good, good. Lotta good.* He continued to
dissolve his brain a little longer, but was inevitably interrupted.

* * * 

Drool flew off his face when someone slapped him hard on the back of his head.
An unconscious chuckle escaped his smiling lips as he slammed face-first into
the window. His obnoxious tinted glasses slid up through his messy black hair.

"Fucking brainrotted bastard. Can you hear me? Manoj needs you, we're heading
out in five," Habib commanded.

Elliot shook off all but the news and split his focus properly: half his mind
resided in the Matrix, and half resided in the room with Habib and the others.
He reset his glasses, twisted elegantly, back to the server room, and looked up
at the large man with an even greater smirk.
