### Chapter 1: Elliot: The Cipherists

<!-- Scene: kern-marez-0934 -->
<!-- Characters: Elliot, Manoj, Rafael, Habib, Anita-->
<!-- Date: December 1, 2103 -->

The server room Elliot Fetterman overlooked was molten, dry air perturbed by the
heat, no light other than the muted red glow of stacked metal. The entire array
was sunken down into the ground, his view obscured by the reinforced metal
framing of a thick acrylic window, tilted overlooking the expanse. Each node
flickered, and the whole valley of computers faded into the dark fog of the
background.

Elliot sat with one leg propped up on the windowsill, and the other idly
scraping the side of the wall as it swung. Lost in a waking dream, twice
embodied the doubleSignal gazed at nothing.

Slip into waking dream.

A cultured steak sat half-eaten on Manoj's lap.

Red lines from Anita's wrist.
